                                                                        There are several potential solutions to overcome the limitations

                                                                        DocDiff is able to generate sharp text edges in most cases, there
                                                                        are still instances where characters appear erroneous or distorted.
                                                                        To address this issue, one approach is to utilize the text prior in
                                                                        order to incorporate additional semantic information. Currently,
